A Little Message

Some words are difficult to say directly, so I wrote them here.

Kuch dino se baat nahi ho pa rahi hai. Kabhi-kabhi phone uthata hoon aur sochta hoon shayad tumhara message aaya ho.

Abhi hum thode door hain, aur milna bhi possible nahi ho pa raha. Lekin umeed hai ki kuch dino mein sab theek hoga aur milne ka mauka bhi milega.

Bas itna kehna tha — apna khayal rakhna, aur jab bhi time mile, ek message kar dena.
